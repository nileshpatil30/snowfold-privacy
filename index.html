Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

techsnort ("we") makes Snowfold, a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stars, completed levels, your place in Endless and your snowflake wall) and your settings (sound on or off) are saved on your phone only, in the app's own storage. Test versions of the app also keep the counts shown on the "Playtest" screen there. We cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Sharing

We do not share user data with anyone, because we do not collect any. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no other third-party code that collects data.

Network use

The app does not send or receive data over the internet. The fonts, artwork and levels are inside the app.

Retention and deletion

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. What the app saves on your phone stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

"Challenge a friend" codes and result text

The code is text that the app creates from your folds and snips, or from a paper you folded. The app does not send it anywhere. If you copy a code or a result and share it with a friend, you do that through the messaging app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the install, such as device type and crash reports, under Google's own policies. We do not receive your personal information from Google.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever changes what it collects, we will update this page and the app's Data safety section on Google Play before the change reaches users.

Contact

For questions or requests about privacy, use the contact email shown on the Snowfold page on Google Play.
